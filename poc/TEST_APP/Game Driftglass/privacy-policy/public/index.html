Driftglass Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · App: Driftglass: Live Home Screen (package com.driftglass.home) · Developer: Strategic Access Ventures

In short. Driftglass has no accounts. Your wallpapers, favorites and settings stay on your phone, and wallpapers are generated on the device. To work, the app connects to our app service and to Google and TikTok services. Driftglass is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings.

1. What Driftglass does

Driftglass shows animated wallpapers that are drawn in real time on your device, lets you create your own in Studio, changes them by time of day or on a schedule, can set a still frame as your system or lock screen wallpaper, and can work as your Home screen (launcher).

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No contacts, location permission, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not upload your wallpapers, settings or app list anywhere.

3. Information processed only on your device

  • Wallpapers and settings. The wallpapers you create, your favorites, schedules and settings are stored in the app’s private storage.
  • App list (Home screen mode). To show your apps and sort them into folders, the Driftglass launcher reads the list of installed apps, their icons and their app category on your device. This list is not sent anywhere.
  • Motion sensor. If parallax is on, the wallpaper reacts to the tilt of your phone using the accelerometer. The readings are used only to draw the picture and are not stored.
  • Battery level. Driftglass checks the battery level to slow the animation down and save power. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

4. Network connections

  • Our app service. Driftglass connects to our service to receive app settings and content and to measure how the app is used. It sends a random app instance ID created on install, the install source reported by Google Play (for example, which campaign brought the install), the app version, the time and basic app events (such as app opened, screen viewed, notification opened), and a notification token if notifications are on. It does not send your wallpapers, settings or app list. This data is used to run the app, to understand which features and campaigns work, and to send notifications.
  • Google Firebase. Driftglass uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications. Firebase uses a Firebase installation ID and a messaging token. See Firebase privacy and security and Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok Business SDK. To measure our advertising campaigns on TikTok, Driftglass may use the TikTok Business SDK, which receives app events (such as app install and app open), your device’s advertising ID, IP address and basic device information. See TikTok’s Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Driftglass is free and shows ads inside the app, for example a full-screen ad when you open the app or move between its screens, and occasionally after you apply or save wallpapers.

  • What AdMob uses. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device’s advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information (such as model, OS version and language), and how you interact with ads (views and taps). Google processes this data under its own policies.
  • Personalized ads. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. To opt out, open Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose “Delete advertising ID” or “Opt out of Ads Personalization”. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Learn more. How Google uses information from apps that use its services and How Google uses advertising data.

We, Strategic Access Ventures, do not receive your advertising ID from AdMob; we only see aggregated ad reports.

6. Notifications

With your permission, Driftglass can send notifications, for example when new wallpapers arrive or as a reminder to come back. You can turn them off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Driftglass → Notifications.

7. Home screen (launcher) role

Driftglass can be set as your Home screen. You choose this in an Android system dialog, and you can turn it off at any time in Driftglass → Settings → “Use as Home screen”, or in Android Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app.

To uninstall Driftglass, use Android Settings → Apps → Driftglass → Uninstall. Depending on the version of the app, you can also long-press an app icon on the Driftglass Home screen and choose Uninstall.

8. System permissions

  • Notifications — only if you allow them (see section 6).
  • Set wallpaper — only when you choose to set a still frame as your system or lock screen wallpaper.
  • Request delete packages — only to open Android’s own uninstall dialog when you choose Uninstall on the Driftglass Home screen. Android always asks you to confirm.
  • Advertising ID — lets Google AdMob and TikTok read the device’s advertising ID (see sections 4 and 5).
  • Internet, network state, vibration — to connect to the services above, check connectivity and give tap feedback.

9. Children

Driftglass is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in Driftglass are not designed for children.

10. Data retention and deletion

Your wallpapers and settings stay on your device and you control them: delete wallpapers you created in the app, or uninstall Driftglass to remove its private storage. App instance IDs and app events on our service are kept only as long as needed to run the app and understand how it is used; to ask us to delete data linked to your app instance, write to the address below. Data handled by Google and TikTok follows their policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings at any time.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s store listing.

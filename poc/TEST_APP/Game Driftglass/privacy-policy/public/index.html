Driftglass Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · App: Driftglass: Live Home Screen (package com.driftglass.home) · Developer: Strategic Access Ventures

In short. Driftglass has no accounts. Your wallpapers, favorites and settings stay on your phone, and wallpapers are generated on the device. The app uses Google Firebase services to work correctly and Google AdMob to show ads, which keep Driftglass free. AdMob uses your device’s advertising ID; you can reset it or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings.

1. What Driftglass does

Driftglass shows animated wallpapers that are drawn in real time on your device, lets you create your own in Studio, changes them by time of day or on a schedule, can set a still frame as your system or lock screen wallpaper, and can work as your Home screen (launcher).

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No contacts, location permission, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • No analytics SDK in the app.

3. Information processed only on your device

  • Wallpapers and settings. The wallpapers you create, your favorites, schedules and settings are stored in the app’s private storage.
  • App list (Home screen mode). To show your apps and sort them into folders, the Driftglass launcher reads the list of installed apps, their icons and their app category on your device. This list is not sent anywhere.
  • Motion sensor. If parallax is on, the wallpaper reacts to the tilt of your phone using the accelerometer. The readings are used only to draw the picture and are not stored.
  • Battery level. Driftglass checks the battery level to slow the animation down and save power. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

4. Network connections

  • Google Firebase. Driftglass uses Google Firebase to keep the app working correctly. Firebase uses a Firebase installation ID and basic technical data (app version, device language, country derived from the IP address). See Firebase privacy and security and Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Driftglass is free and shows ads from Google AdMob inside the app: a full-screen ad when you open or return to the app, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you apply or save wallpapers. Ads are not shown on the Home screen itself and not more often than every few minutes.

  • What AdMob uses. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device’s advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information (such as model, OS version and language), and how you interact with ads (views and taps). Google processes this data under its own policies.
  • Personalized ads. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. To opt out, open Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose “Delete advertising ID” or “Opt out of Ads Personalization”. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Learn more. How Google uses information from apps that use its services and How Google uses advertising data.

We, Strategic Access Ventures, do not receive your advertising ID or any personal information from AdMob; we only see aggregated ad reports.

6. Home screen (launcher) role

Driftglass can be set as your Home screen. You choose this in an Android system dialog, and you can turn it off at any time in Driftglass → Settings → “Use as Home screen”, or in Android Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app.

To uninstall Driftglass, use Android Settings → Apps → Driftglass → Uninstall. Depending on the version of the app, you can also long-press an app icon on the Driftglass Home screen and choose Uninstall.

7. System permissions

  • Set wallpaper — only when you choose to set a still frame as your system or lock screen wallpaper.
  • Request delete packages — only to open Android’s own uninstall dialog when you choose Uninstall on the Driftglass Home screen. Android always asks you to confirm.
  • Advertising ID — lets Google AdMob read the device’s advertising ID to show ads (see section 5).
  • Internet, network state, vibration — to connect to Google services and ads, check connectivity, and give tap feedback.

8. Children

Driftglass is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in Driftglass are not designed for children.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because your data stays on your device, you control it. Delete wallpapers you created in the app, or uninstall Driftglass to remove its private storage. Firebase and AdMob data are handled by Google as described in its policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s store listing.
